This is basically Kirzi's method of visualizing their draft options. Click a mon (or two, or three), then other mons will change color -- blue means that mon is looking like a very good choice, green also being good, yellow is bad, and red is very bad.

Note that this is designed specifically for counterpicking as a carry vs other carries, generally in the 5th draft slot. It doesn't factor in team synergy on either side, doesn't factor in (most?) tank/support matchups, lane matchups, or anything more complex than basic matchups. Thus, the intended use is more to narrow down options by eliminating bad matchups -- not so much trying to draft the perfect mon, because ultimately you need to use your brain and consider those other factors yourself.
