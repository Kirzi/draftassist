This is basically Kirzi's method of visualizing their draft options. Click a mon (or two, or three), then other mons will change color -- blue means that mon is looking like a very good choice into the mons you clicked, green also being good, yellow is bad, and red is very bad.

At least in theory, anyway. This is very much still a work in progress, and only "works" for a few mons at the moment.

Note that this is designed specifically for counterpicking as a carry vs other carries, generally in the 5th draft slot. It doesn't factor in team synergy on either side, lane matchups, or any sort of complex draft strategy. Thus, the intended use is more to narrow down options by eliminating bad matchups and highlighting potentially viable options you may not have considered otherwise -- not so much trying to conclusively draft the ideal mon, because ultimately you need to use your brain and consider those other factors yourself.
